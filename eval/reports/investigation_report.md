## Example narration (inv_late_01)

> March 2018 was genuinely elevated: 19.0% of delivered orders were late versus a 5.8% baseline, with the gap concentrated in carrier transit time.

> - Late rate was 19.0% against a 5.8% baseline, a gap of 13.2 percentage points, across 7,003 delivered orders (1,328 late).
> - Transit days averaged 12.97 versus a 9.06-day baseline, about 3.9 days longer, while handling days were close to baseline (3.33 vs 3.19) and promised days were slightly shorter than baseline (22.03 vs 23.78).
> - The pattern was systemic rather than confined to a few states: the late rate excluding the top two states was still 23.7%.
> - RJ stood out with a 34.5% late rate versus a 10.5% baseline (864 orders, 208 excess late orders), followed by SP at 9.4% versus 4.1% and MG at 20.4% versus 3.3%.
