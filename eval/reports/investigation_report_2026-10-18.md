> March 2018 was genuinely elevated for late deliveries: 19.0% of the 7,003 delivered orders were late versus a 5.8% baseline, a gap of 13.2 percentage points, and the pattern was systemic rather than confined to a few states or sellers.

> - Late rate was 19.0% against a 5.8% baseline (delta 13.2 points), with 1,328 late orders out of 7,003 delivered.
> - The top driver was carrier_transit: transit averaged 12.97 days versus a 9.06-day baseline, roughly 3.9 days longer, while handling was only slightly above baseline (3.33 vs 3.19 days).
> - The problem was systemic: excluding the two worst states still left a 23.7% late rate, and late orders spanned 466 sellers with the top 5 accounting for only 11.5% of them.
> - It was concentrated geographically: RJ ran 34.5% late (baseline 10.5%, 208 excess late orders), MG 20.4% (baseline 3.3%, 147 excess), and SP 9.4% (baseline 4.1%, 158 excess).
